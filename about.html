Hi, I'm Lena.

A fashion illustrator and live event artist based in Berlin.

I’ve always had a lot of interests, but art and design is the thread that runs through all of them. My path into illustration didn’t lead through art school but through branding. Earlier in my career, I led creative teams and projects at tech companies and startups.

At the fashion brand tausendkind (now part of vertbaudet), I was responsible for brand content, including fashion shoots. I led a major global rebrand for the tech startup fotograf.de, and I co-created the travel app By the Ways.

Today, I specialize in live drawing. It’s fast and a little unpredictable, which I love, and it gets me out from behind my desk and into the most unique places. But what I love most about my job is the big smile on people’s faces when they receive their own personal work of art.

When I’m not drawing, you’ll probably find me exploring Berlin with my family or heading off on a road trip whenever we get the chance.

Let's turn it into something people will notice.

Drop me an email — German or English. I usually respond within a few hours.
