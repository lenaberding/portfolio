The magic of a live sketch

A portrait may be small enough to take home in your pocket, but it’s a big gesture. It means a lot when someone takes the time to create something just for you. And seeing the magic happen right in front of you makes the feelings go through the roof.

So here's the plan

01

Preparation

A call about your creative brief or challenge. It can be a product launch, a VIP dinner, or creating social media mentions for your brand. We discuss the goals, the number of guests, the schedule, and any custom requests, so I can prepare everything for the event.

02

Event

Live sketching sessions usually last from 3 hours to a full day. I take a quick photo of each guest, so they are free to enjoy the event while I draw. In 5 minutes, I create an artistic fashion sketch for them to take home!

03

Results

A memorable event and a personal illustration your guests take home, and share on social media. Optionally, I create a custom event illustration or digitise selected sketches after the event.
